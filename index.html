Swanies Factory

Swanies Factory is a private desktop program used only by the creators of the YouTube channel The Swanies Doing Stuff.

It edits our own e-bike ride footage into finished videos on our own computer. When we approve a finished video, the program uploads it, its thumbnail and its Shorts to our own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.

It is not a public service: no one else can sign in to it or use it, and it does not read or collect anyone else's data.
