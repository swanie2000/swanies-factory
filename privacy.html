Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Swanies Factory is a private desktop program that uploads videos made by the owners of the YouTube channel The Swanies Doing Stuff to that channel. Only the channel owners use it.

YouTube API Services

Swanies Factory uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy policy applies to data handled by Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

What data it uses

  • A Google sign-in token, with permission only to upload videos (the youtube.upload scope), granted by the channel owner.
  • The videos, thumbnails, titles and descriptions the channel owners made and chose to upload.

It does not read, collect or store any information about viewers or other YouTube users, and it does not use cookies or analytics.

How the data is used and stored

The sign-in token is stored only on the channel owners' own computer and is used only to upload their own approved videos and thumbnails to their own channel. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's YouTube API. No data is shared with, or sold to, anyone.

Removing access

The channel owner can delete the stored token from their computer at any time, and can revoke the program's access at any time on the Google security settings page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the project's GitHub page.
